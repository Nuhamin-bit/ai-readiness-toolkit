# Session Guides

Four 60-minute sessions, one per week. Safe use comes first, because people start using AI on day one.

## Session 1: Diligence

**Goal:** staff can use AI without exposing private data.

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–10 min | Why safe use comes first. Share one real example of an AI data leak or mistake in the news. |
| 10–30 min | Walk through the [safe-use policy](safe-use-policy.md) together. Fill in the approved tools and contact person. |
| 30–50 min | **Practice:** sort 10 sample requests into "OK for AI" and "Not OK." Discuss the tricky ones. |
| 50–60 min | Everyone acknowledges the policy. Questions. |

**Facilitator tip:** make reporting mistakes safe. People who fear blame hide problems.

## Session 2: Description

**Goal:** staff get a useful first draft in one or two tries.

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–15 min | The four parts of a good request: **task, context, audience, format**. Show a weak and a strong request side by side. |
| 15–25 min | How to fix a weak answer: say what was wrong ("too long," "too formal") instead of starting over. |
| 25–50 min | **Practice:** each person rewrites one chosen use case as a four-part request and compares results. |
| 50–60 min | Add the best requests to a shared prompt library. |

> **Weak:** "Summarize this."
> **Strong:** "Summarize this report in 5 bullet points for our board, who aren't technical. Flag any decisions they need to make."

## Session 3: Delegation

**Goal:** staff can decide what to hand to AI and what to keep.

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–15 min | What AI does well (drafting, summarizing, reformatting) and what stays with people (decisions about people, money, legal and safety matters). |
| 15–45 min | **Practice:** map one chosen use case into steps. Mark each step "AI drafts," "Person does" or "Person checks." |
| 45–60 min | Share maps. Agree on one new workflow to try this week. |

## Session 4: Discernment

**Goal:** staff catch AI mistakes before they spread.

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–15 min | How AI gets things wrong: invented facts and sources, outdated information, confident tone, bias. |
| 15–40 min | **Practice:** find the errors in three prepared AI answers, each with one planted mistake. |
| 40–50 min | Checking habits: open every source, double-check numbers and names, ask a person when stakes are high. |
| 50–60 min | Everyone retakes the readiness check. |

**Preparing the practice answers:** ask an AI tool a question you know the answer to, then edit in one wrong number, one fake source or one wrong name. Keep an answer key.
